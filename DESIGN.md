# Tani-Awas Design System (Clean Editorial / Swiss Agro-Science)

## 1. Visual Theme & Philosophy
- **Archetype**: Clean Editorial Agro-Intelligence Platform (Nature/The Pudding Meets European Drought Observatory).
- **Tone**: Grounded, authoritative, calm, high legibility for agricultural researchers, extension officers, and academic competition juries.
- **Surface Philosophy**: Warm paper canvas (`#fbfcf8` / `#f4f6f0`), pure crisp white containers (`#ffffff`), structural hairline borders (`#e2e8f0` / `#d1d5db`), zero artificial neon glows or cyberpunk floating orbs.
- **Accent Philosophy**: Deep botanical pine green (`#1b4332` / `#2d6a4f`), warm earth terracotta for alert (`#b91c1c`), wheat gold for warning (`#b45309`), and fresh meadow green for safe baseline (`#15803d`).

## 2. Color Tokens
- `--bg-canvas`: `#f8faf7` (Warm organic off-white)
- `--bg-surface`: `#ffffff` (Pure crisp white cards)
- `--bg-surface-subtle`: `#f1f5f0` (Warm subtle gray-green tint)
- `--border-subtle`: `#e5e7eb` (1px clean structural border)
- `--border-strong`: `#d1d5db` (Interactive input border)
- `--text-primary`: `#111827` (Deep slate black, ultra-clear contrast)
- `--text-secondary`: `#4b5563` (Balanced readable body)
- `--text-muted`: `#6b7280` (Auxiliary metadata & units)
- `--brand-primary`: `#1b4332` (Deep agricultural forest green)
- `--brand-accent`: `#2d6a4f` (Vibrant botanical leaf)
- `--status-danger`: `#b91c1c` (Deep crimson red)
- `--status-danger-bg`: `#fef2f2`
- `--status-danger-border`: `#fecaca`
- `--status-warning`: `#b45309` (Warm amber brown)
- `--status-warning-bg`: `#fffbeb`
- `--status-warning-border`: `#fde68a`

- `--status-safe-bg`: `#f0fdf4`

- `--chart-ndvi`: `#2d6a4f` (Forest green line)

## 3. Typography & Numerics
- **Font Stack**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, -apple-system, sans-serif.
- **Monospace Stack**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (strictly limited to numbers, coordinates, and percentages with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Headings**: Editorial serif/clean sans balance with natural sentence case. No aggressive all-caps screaming.

## 4. Anti-Slop Enforcements
- No fake pulsing radar circles.
- No dark sci-fi spaceship control panels.
- Real interactive calculations on all sliders and inputs.
