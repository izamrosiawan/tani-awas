# Design Brief: TANI-AWAS (Agro-Climatic Intelligence & Food Security Platform)

## Design Read
An authoritative, cinematic national agro-climatic intelligence platform for agricultural officers, irrigation managers, and policy makers monitoring drought, crop health, and food security across Indonesian rice production basins.

## Concept Spine
**The Living Biosphere Dossier**: The interface operates like a high-altitude agro-spectral sensor system, descending from macro-satellite orbits through regional cloud layers and precipitation radars, directly down to field-level AWD hydrologic controls and poktan harvest security.

## Delivery Tier
`cinema` (Lenis smooth physics, ambient atmospheric perspective, seamless multi-chapter scroll journey, responsive mathematical telemetry).

## Animation mode
Animation mode: animated-website

### Journey Architecture (A4 - Single-Shot Atmospheric Odyssey)
- **Journey shape**: `single-shot`
- **Chapter 1: Orbital Multispectral Survey** — Satellite Sentinel-2 scanning Indonesia's rice basins from space, calculating vegetative indices across 25,000+ Ha.
- **Chapter 2: Atmospheric Deficit Descent** — Penetrating tropospheric moisture layers, mapping BMKG rainfall deficit gradients and land surface temperatures.
- **Chapter 3: Regional Risk Telemetry Grid** — Radar telemetry locking onto high-risk districts (Karanganyar Demak, Indramayu, Subang, Sragen) with real-time bio-physical indices.
- **Chapter 4: Ground-Level Precision Hydrology** — Direct deployment of AWD (Alternate Wetting and Drying) solar water pumps, fuel logistics, and water volume conservation.
- **Chapter 5: Food Sovereignty & AUTP Guarantee** — Final harvest yield defense matrix and crop failure compensation simulator under Kementan & Jasindo protocols.

## Section Plan
1. **Hero Cinematic Station** (`full-viewport-camera`): Interactive agro-climatic globe/orbit visual with live Sentinel-2 sensor status, macro KPIs, and immediate mission prompt.
2. **Interactive Telemetry Dashboard** (`split-instrument-cockpit`): Real-time district switcher (Demak, Indramayu, Karanganyar, Grobogan, etc.) with dynamic NDVI charts, rain deficit curves, and crop risk scores.
3. **Operational Hydraulic Engine** (`bento-utility-grid`): Real AWD water pump volume calculators, fuel requirement modeling, and soil moisture mitigation recommendations.
4. **Institutional AUTP Claim Simulator** (`analytical-card-matrix`): Official Ministry of Agriculture (Kementan) 75% puso damage threshold validation and financial relief payout modeling.
5. **Field Verification & Action Stream** (`terminal-feed-dossier`): Field reports, poktan monitoring nodes, and immediate dispatch directives.
